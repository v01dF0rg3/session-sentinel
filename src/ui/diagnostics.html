Diagnostics

Exercises the browser APIs Session Sentinel depends on and reports what actually works here. It does not delete real website data. It reads cookie metadata, creates two public dummy cookies on a reserved .invalid test domain, then removes them and checks the result. Any leftover test cookies expire within two minutes.

Why this page exists. The logic that decides what to delete is covered by automated tests, but the browser APIs that do the deleting can only be exercised inside a real installed extension. This is how we find out whether they behave on your Chrome before you trust the extension with a real account.

CheckResult

Private-store account check

Tests whether a site gives anonymous visitors the same cookie names: load one site in a genuinely empty Incognito cookie store and compare names with the normal profile. It never reads or reports cookie values. The site receives one ordinary private visit. The result is diagnostic only: sites may reuse the same cookie name before and after sign-in, so no account verdict is saved and the account list is not changed.

Before each run, close every Incognito window and open exactly one fresh blank window. Do not use it during the test. Close it afterwards to erase the temporary private cookies and site data.

Coverage

How often the extension reaches a site's sign-out route or control instead of only deleting local data. Reaching it is useful, but does not prove the server rejected a copied token. This evidence identifies the sites most worth improving; it is not a count of accounts proven safe.
