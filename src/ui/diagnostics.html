Diagnostics

Exercises the browser APIs Session Sentinel depends on and reports what actually works here. Safe to run: it reads your cookie list but does not delete any of it, and the only data it clears belongs to a reserved test domain that cannot exist.

Why this page exists. The logic that decides what to delete is covered by automated tests, but the browser APIs that do the deleting can only be exercised inside a real installed extension. This is how we find out whether they behave on your Chrome before you trust the extension with a real account.

CheckResult

Activity log

Every step of every run, kept across browser restarts. If Chrome closes during a logout, this shows exactly where it got to — look for whether run:complete appears before the next browser:startup.
