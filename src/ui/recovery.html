Account recovery checklist

Review the highest-impact accounts first, then work down the list.

If malware may still be on this computer, stop here. Disconnect the affected computer and continue from another trusted phone or computer. Do not enter a new password or recovery code on a device that may still be infected.
For each account: review active sessions or devices and remove anything unfamiliar; use sign out everywhere if the site offers it; change the password if it may be exposed; then review two-step verification, recovery methods, connected apps, and backup codes. A password change does not guarantee every session is closed. Nothing on this page acts automatically.
