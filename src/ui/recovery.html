Securing your accounts

Work down this list. It is ordered so that each account you secure stays secured.

Change the password — do not just log out. If someone else is using your accounts, logging yourself out gives up the session you control and leaves theirs running. Changing the password ends every other session at once and keeps you signed in. Nothing on this page logs you out of anything.
