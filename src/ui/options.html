Session Sentinel

Everything below is stored on this computer. The extension has no account, sends no telemetry, and never transmits your browsing history anywhere.

What "log out" can and cannot do. Deleting cookies protects you on this machine. It does not invalidate a token that was already stolen. Where a site offers a real "sign out of all devices" control, Session Sentinel drives it in a hidden tab and reports revoked; otherwise it reports cleared locally and means it.

Automatic triggers

When the browser closes

Chrome gives extensions no reliable shutdown hook, so this runs on close if it can and otherwise completes on next launch, before your restored tabs load. Sessions are gone by the time the browser is usable again.

After inactivity

Applies to sites at or above the selected risk level.

min

When the screen locks or sleeps

A deliberate "I am walking away" signal, so it is treated more aggressively.

How hard to try

Contact sites to invalidate sessions

Opens a hidden background window to use the site's own sign-out, which is the only way to kill a session server-side. Costs a few seconds per site.

Per-site data removal

Deep also clears IndexedDB and cache storage. Some sites keep drafts and offline documents there, so deep is reserved for the tiers where that is unlikely.

Your open tabs

A page you already have open keeps its session in memory, so it carries on looking signed in until it is reloaded — even though the logout worked. Reloading it makes the result visible immediately. Turn this off if you would rather refresh pages yourself; note that reloading discards anything you had typed into a form on that site.

Show a notification after each run

Reports what was revoked, what was only cleared, and what failed.

Logout recipes

Keep site recipes up to date

Sites move their "sign out everywhere" buttons, and a stale recipe quietly downgrades a site from revoked to cleared locally. Updates are signed, and the whole list is downloaded at once — never queried per site — so the server cannot learn which sites you use. If an update fails or cannot be verified, the recipes built into the extension stay in force.

Sites

Set a site to never clear to stay signed in there — it is skipped by every automatic trigger and by "Log out of all sessions". You can still clear it by hand from the popup. Always protect does the opposite: the site is treated as critical no matter how it was classified.
